How it works

InfoLedgr is a website that works like an app. Everything you record is kept on your device and copied to your own Google Sheet, which keeps your phone and laptop in sync and doubles as your backup.

4Turn it on (deploy)This lets the app talk to your Sheet. You only do this once.
  1. In your Sheet, click Extensions → Apps Script. A new tab opens with code. Don't change anything there.
  2. Top right: click the blue Deploy button → New deployment
  3. Next to "Select type", click the ⚙️ gear → Web app
  4. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone → click Deploy

5Connect the app to your SheetBack in your Sheet's tab
  1. Click InfoLedgr → 2. Show connection details
  2. If it asks for your Web app URL, paste the one you copied in step 4 → OK
  3. A box shows your one-tap connect link. Click inside it, copy it (Ctrl+C, on Mac ⌘+C)
  4. Paste it into your browser's address bar and press Enter
  5. InfoLedgr opens with your details filled in → click Connect & sync
✅
You'll see "Connected — your data is syncing ✓", and the sync button gets a green dot.
🔒
Keep your connect link private. It unlocks your data, like a key. Only send it to yourself.
After opening your connect link
6Put InfoLedgr on your phoneSo it opens like a normal app
  1. Send your connect link to yourself, e.g. in WhatsApp "Message yourself", or by email
  2. On your phone, open the link → tap Connect & sync

🍎 iPhone (Safari)

If the link opened in WhatsApp's browser, tap ⋯ → Open in Safari first.
Then tap the Share button ⬆️ → scroll down → Add to Home Screen → Add.

🤖 Android (Chrome)

If the link opened inside WhatsApp, tap ⋮ → Open in Chrome first.
Then tap ⋮ (top right) → Add to Home screen or Install app → Install.

💡
Using a second laptop or tablet too? Just open the same connect link on it. Everything stays in sync.

If something doesn't work

What you seeWhat to do
No "InfoLedgr" menu in the SheetWait 10 seconds and reload the page. Make sure you're in your copy, not the template.
"Google hasn't verified this app"Normal for your own copy: Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow (step 3).
"Could not connect: Failed to fetch"In Apps Script: Deploy → Manage deployments → ✏️ → Who has access: Anyone → Deploy. Check the URL ends in /exec.
"The key doesn't match this Web app URL"The URL and key came from different Sheets. Use InfoLedgr → 2. Show connection details in your Sheet, and copy the URL from Apps Script opened from that Sheet (Extensions → Apps Script).
"That Web app URL belongs to a different Sheet"Same fix as above: open Apps Script from inside your own Sheet and copy its URL.
Phone shows old dataTap the sync button (circular arrows) at the top, or close and reopen the app.
